HOW TO PLAY

ANIME CONQUER

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Eliminate all other players by destroying every unit in their hand and on the board. Last player standing wins.

🗺️ THE BOARD

6×6 grid. Each player starts in a corner. Tiles scale in strength toward the centre:

  • Outer ring — Common & Rare
  • Middle ring — Epic & Mythic
  • Centre 4 tiles — Legendary

🃏 YOUR HAND

You start with 3 cards. If all your cards (hand + board) are gone, you are eliminated.

⚡ 3 ACTION POINTS PER TURN

  • Place — 1 AP
  • Move — 1 AP
  • Attack — 2 AP
  • Trade — 2 AP
  • Awaken — 1 AP

⚔️ BATTLE

Both units appear in a battle modal. Players decide the winner — the loser is removed.

🌐 ONLINE MODES

  • Standard — Auto-resolve battles by tier power
  • Voting — Both players vote who wins; ties go to AI

In both online modes, opponent hands are hidden.

👥 LOCAL MODE

A "Pass Device" screen appears between turns so each player only sees their own hand.

💱 TRADING

  • 5 cards → Legendary
  • Naruto/MHA Ultra: 4+1 leg, 2+2 leg, or 3 leg
  • Demon Slayer Ultra: 3 legendary
  • One Piece Ultra: 2 leg or 5 mythic

🏆 SHOWDOWN MODE

Race to claim all neutral tiles. A 3-turn countdown then triggers an all-vs-all showdown based on board units.

✨ AWAKENING

Units marked with ⚡ can evolve. Select the unit, pick sacrifice cards, confirm. In 2-player games only base forms spawn.
