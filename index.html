HOW TO PLAY

ANIME CONQUER

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Eliminate all other players by destroying every unit in their hand and on the board. Last player standing wins.

🗺️ THE BOARD

The game is played on a 6×6 grid. Each player starts in a corner (their base). Neutral tiles scale in strength toward the centre:

  • Outer ring — Common & Rare (tiles bordering the middle ring lean Rare)
  • Middle ring — Epic & Mythic (tiles closest to centre lean Mythic)
  • Centre 4 tiles — Legendary

💡 The board is reshuffled every game — same zones, different characters every time.

🃏 YOUR HAND

You start with 3 cards. If all your cards (hand + board) are gone, you are eliminated.

⚡ YOUR TURN — 3 ACTION POINTS

Each turn you have 3 AP (shown as glowing dots above the board). Spend them how you like:

  • Place a card — 2 AP · Deploy from hand to your base or owned tile
  • Move a unit — 1 AP · Slide a board unit to an adjacent empty tile
  • Attack — 2 AP · Send a unit against an adjacent enemy
  • Trade — 2 AP · Select cards (yellow glow), click TRADE for a rarer card
  • Awaken / Evolve — 1 AP · Evolve a unit marked with ⚡

💡 Example: Move + Move + Move (3×1 AP) OR Place + Move (2+1 AP) in one turn. Click END TURN to skip remaining AP.

⚔️ BATTLE

Both units appear in a battle modal. Players decide the winner — the loser is removed. Attacking a neutral unit claims it for your team.

💱 TRADING

  • 5 cards → Legendary
  • Naruto / MHA Ultra: 4+1 leg, 2+2 leg, or 3 leg → Ultra
  • Demon Slayer Ultra: 3 legendary → Ultra
  • One Piece Ultra: 2 legendaries or 5 mythics → Ultra
  • One Piece GOD: 1 ultra + 3 legendaries + 1 any → God Tier

💡 The Trade Guide next to the board shows live pool counts during the game.

✨ AWAKENING

Some units can evolve into stronger forms. Select a unit marked with ⚡ on the board or in your hand, then click the evolve button that appears. Choose the required sacrifice cards from your hand and confirm.

  • Naruto — Button says AWAKEN. Shouts "SHINRA TENSEI!" on activation. Chains include: Naruto (Base → Sage → KCM → Six Paths), Sasuke, Madara, Kakashi, Guy, Obito, Minato, Hashirama.
  • One Piece — Button says AWAKEN. Shouts "AWAKENING!". Chains include: Luffy (5 forms up to Gear 5), Kaido, Big Mom, Blackbeard, Zoro, Sanji, Law, Kid, Katakuri, Sabo, Robin, Doflamingo, and more.
  • Bleach — Button says BANKAI. Shouts "BANKAI!". Chains include: Ichigo (Shikai → Bankai → Hollow Mask → True Bankai), Byakuya, Zaraki, Urahara, Unohana, Yoruichi, Rukia, Hitsugaya, Grimmjow, Ulquiorra (Segunda Etapa), Aizen, Yhwach, Yamamoto, and more.
  • MHA — Button says AWAKEN. Chains include Plus Ultra evolutions for key heroes.

💡 In 2-player games only base forms spawn — evolve them yourself for maximum power.

🏆 SHOWDOWN MODE

Choose Showdown in the game mode selector before starting. The game plays like Conquest — but instead of eliminating each other, players race to claim neutral tiles. Once all neutral tiles on the board are owned by a player, a 3-turn countdown begins. After those 3 turns, the SHOWDOWN triggers automatically:

  • Every player's board units are displayed in their column
  • All units fight simultaneously — all vs all
  • Players look at the rosters and declare the winner based on combined strength
  • Only units on the board count — hand cards do not fight in the showdown

💡 Strategy: prioritize claiming tiles over attacking opponents. Stronger tiles = stronger showdown roster.

💡 TIPS

  • Hover cards or tiles to see a large preview
  • Control the center — legendary tiles are the biggest power spikes
  • A full hand = more trading power, don't neglect drawing
  • Awaken at the front line for maximum pressure
